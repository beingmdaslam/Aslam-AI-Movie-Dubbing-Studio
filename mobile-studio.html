MOBILE SUBTITLE STUDIO

1. Choose video

Your video is previewed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded.

2. Subtitle settings

Translation checks for the browser built-in on-device language models. Support varies by browser and language pair.

Translation is performed with browser-provided language models when available. Unsupported browser/language pairs are reported; no third-party translation API is used.

3. Automatic speech transcription (ASR)

Whisper Tiny runs locally in your browser. The free model downloads on first use; the video is not uploaded. Phone speed and memory limit long-video processing.

Load the model, then transcribe a selected video with an audio track.

4. AI voice generation (TTS)

Local text-to-speech models for supported languages. The voice models are community ONNX conversions of Meta MMS checkpoints; check the original model license before commercial use. First use downloads a model and may require substantial phone storage/RAM.

Choose a supported subtitle language and load its voice model.

DOWNLOAD DUBBED WAV

5. Final MP4 rendering

Mux the generated dubbed WAV with the original video locally in this browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. The original video track is copied and its audio is replaced. First use downloads the free FFmpeg engine; long/high-resolution videos may exceed phone RAM or browser limits.

Generate dubbed WAV first, then render the final MP4.
